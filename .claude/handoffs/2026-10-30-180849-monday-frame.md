## Codebase Understanding
## Architecture Overview
The TV uses device authorization to select a private Krill space, fetches photos and videos from the frame API, and caches its playlist. Cloudflare handles private storage and image transforms; videos use signed Stream playback. A server queue imports Google Picker selections after the browser closes. The Picker opens in its own window because Google does not permit embedding it in an iframe.

- `app/src/main/java/com/neilturner/aerialviews/providers/custom/KrillPrivateProvider.kt`: private feed and authentication.

## Context for Resuming Agent
## Important Context
Footage Rodeo is live. Do not rerun old destructive migrations: Picker migrations 048 through 050 are already applied to the tenant. When Google omits video validators, the uploader verifies each chunk against digests measured from the original. A 1 GiB source and sources ignoring HTTP Range have not been proven on-device. Unit tests and APK builds do not prove live device behavior. The installed TV APK may not contain every change in this branch.

## Assumptions Made
Al wants photos matched to display resolution when the source permits, selective portrait pairings, and videos muted by default with optional remote unmute.

## Potential Gotchas
Wireless ADB ports change after reboot. Videos can initially play a lower rendition before adapting. Never publish personal photos, account identifiers, pairing codes or credentials. Preserve unfinished work rather than discarding it to update upstream.

## Environment State
The VPS Android SDK is under `/opt/android-sdk`. Follow repository test and build guidance. Run Krill heavy pnpm tests and builds through the VPS test wrapper. Production releases use the authorized release wrapper. Keep credentials in the approved secret manager.
